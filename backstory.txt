Arin grew up in a coastal village but developed a deep fear of water after nearly
drowning as a child. Since that incident, Arin has avoided swimming and any
activity involving deep water. This fear shaped many of Arin’s decisions and
limited the places Arin was willing to travel.

During adolescence, Arin made a personal vow to avoid leadership roles, believing
that authority brought suffering and moral compromise. Arin preferred to work
alone and rejected opportunities that involved commanding others.

Arin strongly believes in non-violence and avoids physical confrontation whenever
possible. Even in moments of extreme pressure, Arin chooses withdrawal over
conflict and values personal safety over heroism.
